I launched [drivechain-observatory.xyz](https://drivechain-observatory.xyz) this month ([code](https://github.com/GuiSchet/drivechain-observatory)).

- A live, guided view of drivechains on eCash Betanet: proposals, deposits, withdrawals and merged mining, explained with real data.
- Per-sidechain stats and a downloadable dataset for each sidechain.
- [bip300-monitor](https://github.com/GuiSchet/bip300-monitor) now uses only the official enforcer APIs.
- Getting ready for eCash mainnet (~Oct 31) and my [LABITCONF](https://www.labitconf.com/) workshop on Oct 30.
