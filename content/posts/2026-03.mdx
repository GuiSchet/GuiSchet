A good month for peer-observer, plus a hackathon.

- Merged [#339](https://github.com/peer-observer/peer-observer/pull/339): metrics for how long RPC calls take and how often they fail, with 14 integration tests.
- While working on it I found that the shared metrics server ignores the request path, and reported it in [#384](https://github.com/peer-observer/peer-observer/issues/384).
- Built [c12d](https://github.com/GuiSchet/c12d) at the Bitcoin++ hackathon: real-time analysis and visualization of a Bitcoin Core node.
