This month I started looking at drivechains.

- Started a pilot extractor for the [BIP300/301 enforcer](https://github.com/LayerTwo-Labs/bip300301_enforcer), to record how it sees the chain. The idea came from this [BNOC discussion](https://bnoc.xyz/t/brainstorming-what-data-to-collect-and-monitor-during-the-bip-110-bip-300-forks-in-august-2026/139/12).
- In peer-observer, worked on faster node sync ([infra-library#180](https://github.com/peer-observer/infra-library/issues/180)) and kept going with [#459](https://github.com/peer-observer/peer-observer/pull/459).
- Wrote the design and cost analysis for [Bitcoin Open Data](https://gist.github.com/GuiSchet/08d84ae2b74ed629c7c9d06438e52dcd).
- Went to BitDevs Buenos Aires and gave feedback on [bitdevsmap#5](https://github.com/KyraLabs/bitdevsmap/issues/5).
